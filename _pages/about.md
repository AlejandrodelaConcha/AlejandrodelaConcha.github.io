I am Alejandro de la Concha, a postdoctoral researcher in statistical machine learning at the University of Luxembourg, working in collaboration with Prof. Yannick Baraud. I hold a PhD from École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay.

My work focuses on developing machine learning and statistical methods for complex data generated by transport systems, financial networks, and sensor networks. I have contributed to kernel methods, statistical hypothesis testing, density and likelihood-ratio estimation, and graph-signal processing.

Professionally, I have held positions in public and private institutions, where I have mainly focused on applying machine learning to risk management. I held a research data scientist position at Financial Network Analytics ([FNA](https://fna.fi/)) and a financial risk analyst position at the Central Bank of Mexico ([BANXICO](https://www.banxico.org.mx/indexen.html)) within the Directorate of Risk Management.
